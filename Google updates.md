To update your code manually in Google Sites, follow these steps:

Copy your local code: In your local text editor or IDE, select all your updated code (Ctrl + A or Cmd + A) and copy it (Ctrl + C or Cmd + C).

Open the site editor: Go to your edit URL and ensure you are in the editor view (the URL ends with /edit).

Select the embedded block: Click once on the embedded widget on your page. A floating toolbar will appear above or around the block.

Edit the code:

Click the Pencil (Edit) icon on the floating toolbar.

In the modal that opens, make sure you are on the Embed code tab (not By URL).

Delete the old code completely and paste your updated code into the box.

Save the embed:

Click Next at the bottom right of the modal to see the preview.

Click Save (or Insert).

Publish to make it live:

Look at the top right of the Google Sites header.

Click the blue Publish button.

A side-by-side draft vs. currently published comparison screen may appear. Click Publish again to confirm.

Once published, refresh the live viewing URL (without /edit) in an incognito window or clear the browser cache to confirm the new changes appear.
