Welcome to DuoSpend

Quick start guide & smart features walkthrough

1 Scan or Snap Supermarket Receipts

Tap Scan Receipt to take a photo. DuoSpend's 3-tier OCR automatically detects the store, purchase date, total amount, and itemizes products from tickets (Mercadona, Lidl, Aldi, Carrefour, etc.).

2 Fair Split & Personal Items

Allocate expenses to your joint Home Account or split 50/50. When buying personal items on a shared supermarket trip, tag them as Personal to deduct them from the shared total!

3 Manage Household & Budget

Open Manage Household Members from the top pill or drawer menu to add partners/roommates, customize initial badges and accent colors, and configure monthly sub-budgets.

4 100% Offline & Private Cloud Sync

DuoSpend works offline in poor-signal supermarkets. When connected, sync receipts in real-time across devices via your private Firebase sync room code.
